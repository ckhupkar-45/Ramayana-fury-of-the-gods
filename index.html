The Journey Begins

Ramayana: Fury of the Gods is a mythological action-adventure game inspired by the Vanvas journey of Lord Ram. Explore fascinating environments, face powerful enemies and progress through legendary stages.

An Epic Mythological Journey

The Story

Inspired by the Vanvas arc of the Ramayana, Ramayana: Fury of the Gods takes players into a mythical world inspired by the legendary journey of Lord Ram.

Explore mysterious lands, encounter powerful enemies and overcome challenging battles as you progress through six stages of the adventure.

Explore the Unknown

The Game World

The game world combines fantasy environments, mythological inspiration and stylized 3D design. Each location creates a different atmosphere for exploration and combat.

Magical Forest

A mysterious forest featuring stylized pink trees and a fantasy atmosphere.

Ancient Caves

Explore mysterious caves, including an environment featuring a statue of Lord Shiva.

Floating Islands

Discover five floating islands inspired by the Panchavati environment.

Ram Setu

Experience an environment inspired by the legendary bridge of Ram Setu.

Ravana's Palace

The planned setting for the final encounter with Ravana.

Combat • Exploration • Progression

Gameplay

The game combines open-world-style exploration within individual stages with level-based progression and boss encounters.

Explore

Discover the environments and explore different areas within each stage.

Fight

Face enemies and take on challenging boss encounters.

Progress

Defeat the stage boss to unlock the next level and continue the journey.

Player Interface

The interface displays health, XP, coins, abilities and gameplay controls.

Face Powerful Enemies

Boss Battles

Boss encounters are an important part of the progression system. Players must defeat the boss of a stage before moving forward.

Five Boss Encounters

Five boss encounters have been implemented as part of the game's progression.

Ravana

Ravana was planned as the final boss. His final character model and encounter remain unfinished.

Designed for Immersion

Six Stages

A six-level adventure with stage-based progression.

Day and Night

A changing day-and-night environment that adds atmosphere to the world.

Lighting Effects

Baked lighting effects help create the visual atmosphere of the environments.

Sound Design

Background music and environmental sound effects enhance the experience.

Player HUD

Health, experience, coins and ability indicators support gameplay.

Fantasy Art Style

Stylized environments and distinctive visual choices create a mythical world.

Behind the Project

Development

This project was developed as part of a one-year university game development programme. The work involved environment creation, game design, visual development and gameplay implementation.

Unreal Engine

Used during the development of the game.

CORE

Used to help complete the six-level game within the available development time.

Autodesk Maya

Used for designing 3D elements and supporting the visual development.

The Journey of Creation

Project Reflection

Developing Ramayana: Fury of the Gods was an opportunity to explore game development through environment design, gameplay creation and visual storytelling.

Working within a limited university project timeline required planning, experimentation and decisions about development tools and project scope.

The project represents an important step in my journey as a game developer and a foundation for future creative work.
